Budget 700 mA. Estimated from channel sum at ~60 mA per LED at full white, the same arithmetic pushFrame() uses to clamp.

Layout assumptions

Three boards of 20 LEDs, chained DOUT → DIN. Each carries one corner at its local position 3, so the seams land three LEDs before each corner — between 19|20, 39|40 and 59|0 — and the boards pinwheel rather than meeting at the points. Positions for the five inner LEDs are measured off photographs; the perimeter is treated as three straight runs.
